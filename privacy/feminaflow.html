FeminaFlow Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 3, 2026

1. Introduction

Health data is among the most sensitive information you have. FeminaFlow is built so that your cycle and symptom logs never leave your device at all. This policy explains exactly what that means, and what the app does and does not do.

2. Information We Collect

We collect nothing. FeminaFlow has no account system, no sign-in, and no server. We do not receive your name, email address, phone number, device identifiers, or any other information about you.

What you record stays on your device. Cycles, symptoms, moods and related notes you enter are written to a database in the app's private storage on your phone, and are read only by the app itself.

3. How Your Information is Used

Your entries are used solely to display your tracking and charts inside the app, on your device. They are not used for advertising, analytics, profiling, or research, and are never transmitted anywhere.

4. Permissions

FeminaFlow requests no Android permissions at all. It has no internet permission, so the app is technically incapable of sending your data anywhere. It also does not request access to storage, contacts, location, the camera, or the microphone.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored in the app's private storage area, which other apps on your device cannot read. It is protected by your device's own encryption, which on modern Android is enabled by default and tied to your screen lock.

Please note: FeminaFlow does not currently add a separate PIN or biometric lock of its own. Anyone who can unlock your phone can open the app. Setting a screen lock on your device is therefore the most effective way to protect your logs.

The app is excluded from Android's automatic cloud backup, so your health data is never copied to Google Drive.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

You have full control. You can delete individual entries or clear all data at any time from within the app. Because nothing is stored anywhere else, uninstalling FeminaFlow permanently removes all of your data from your device, and no copy of it exists for anyone to recover.

7. Third-Party Services

FeminaFlow contains no third-party analytics, tracking, advertising, or crash-reporting software. Your data is never shared or sold to insurers, advertisers, or anyone else — there is no mechanism in the app by which it could be.

8. Your Rights (DPDP Act, 2023)

Because FeminaFlow collects no personal data, we hold nothing about you to access, correct, or erase. Your information remains entirely under your control on your own device, and you can view, edit or delete it at any time without contacting anyone.

9. Children's Privacy

FeminaFlow is not intended for use by children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children — consistent with the fact that we collect no information from anyone.

10. Policy Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. Any change will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
